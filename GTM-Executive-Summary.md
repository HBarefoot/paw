# Barefoot Digital GTM Strategy
## Executive Summary (1-Page)

---

## The Opportunity

**Target:** Mid-market logistics operators ($100M-$1B revenue)
**Problem:** Underserved by Big 4 (too expensive) and generic AI consultants (no domain expertise)
**Solution:** Production-ready AI infrastructure shipped in 4-8 weeks, not 6-month pilots

**Market Tailwinds:**
- Labor costs up 23% since 2020
- Legacy system debt: 8-12 disconnected platforms per operator
- AI maturity: LLMs + RAG now reliable for logistics use cases

---

## Value Proposition

> "We ship AI infrastructure that converts **days of manual operations to minutes**, with **95% automation rates** and **$500K+ documented savings** per implementation."

**Differentiation:**
| | Barefoot | Big 4 | Boutique AI | In-House |
|---|----------|-------|-------------|----------|
| Time to Value | 4-8 weeks | 6-12 mo | 8-12 weeks | 18-24 mo |
| Cost | $150K-$300K | $500K-$2M | $200K-$500K | $500K+ |
| Logistics Expertise | Deep | Generalist | Variable | None |
