## LTV Calculation (3-Year Horizon)

### Revenue Stream Breakdown

**Year 1:**
- Implementation: $200K (average)
- Support Contract: $45K
- **Year 1 Total: $245K**

**Year 2:**
- Support Contract: $45K
- Expansion Use Case #1: $75K
- **Year 2 Total: $120K**

**Year 3:**
- Support Contract: $45K
- Expansion Use Case #2: $75K
- **Year 3 Total: $120K**

### Retention Assumptions

| Year | Retention Rate | Cumulative Retention |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| Year 1 | 100% | 100% |
| Year 2 | 90% | 90% |
| Year 3 | 85% | 76.5% |

### Gross Margin Assumptions

- Implementation Work: 65% margin (engineering-heavy)
- Support Contracts: 85% margin (mostly automated monitoring)
- Expansion Work: 70% margin (reusable patterns)
- **Blended Margin: 72%**

### LTV Calculation

```
Year 1: $245K × 100% retention × 72% margin = $176.4K
Year 2: $120K × 90% retention × 72% margin = $77.8K
Year 3: $120K × 76.5% retention × 72% margin = $66.1K

Total LTV = $176.4K + $77.8K + $66.1K = $320.3K
```

**LTV: $320,300**

### LTV:CAC Ratio

```
LTV:CAC = $320,300 / $32,300 = 9.9:1
```

**This is exceptional.** Industry benchmark is 3:1. Indicates:
- Significant room to increase CAC (hire more AEs, increase ad spend)
- Pricing may be too low (could test 20-30% higher)
- Strong product-market fit signal

## Sensitivity Analysis

### Scenario 1: Conservative (50% of target)

| Metric | Base Case | Conservative | Variance |
|--------|-----------|--------------|----------|
| Deals Closed | 5 | 2-3 | -40% to -60% |
| Revenue | $1,225K | $500K | -59% |
| CAC | $32.3K | $50K | +55% |
| LTV:CAC | 9.9:1 | 4.3:1 | -57% |
| Cash Flow (Day 90) | +$47.5K | -$85K | -279% |

**Triggers for conservative scenario:**
- Sales cycle extends to 120+ days
- Win rate drops below 15%
- Content/inbound underperforms by 50%

**Mitigation:**
- Reduce marketing spend by 40% immediately
- Extend runway with founder capital bridge
- Focus on partner-sourced deals (lower CAC)

### Scenario 2: Aggressive (150% of target)

| Metric | Base Case | Aggressive | Variance |
|--------|-----------|------------|----------|
| Deals Closed | 5 | 7-8 | +40% to +60% |
| Revenue | $1,225K | $1,800K | +47% |
| CAC | $32.3K | $28K | -13% |
| LTV:CAC | 9.9:1 | 11.4:1 | +15% |
| Cash Flow (Day 90) | +$47.5K | +$180K | +279% |

**Enablers for aggressive scenario:**
- Viral content piece (10K+ views)
- Major partnership announcement
- Competitor failure creates market opportunity

**Constraints:**
- Engineering capacity (max 3 concurrent implementations)
- Founder bandwidth (BD bottleneck)
- Cash for upfront marketing spend

**Action if aggressive:**
- Hire AE immediately (commission-only trial)
- Increase marketing spend 50%
- Raise prices 15-20% (demand signal)

### Fixed Costs (Monthly)

| Cost | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Engineering (2 FTE) | $40K |
| Founder/BD | $20K |
| Tools/Software | $5K |
| **Total Fixed** | **$65K** |

### Variable Costs (Per Deal)

| Cost | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Marketing (blended CAC) | $32.3K |
| Contractor Support | $15K |
| **Total Variable** | **$47.3K** |

### Contribution Margin Per Deal

```
Avg. Deal Value: $245K
Variable Costs: $47.3K
Contribution Margin: $197.7K
Contribution Margin %: 80.7%
```

### Break-Even Point (Monthly)

```
Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin per Deal
$65K / $197.7K = 0.33 deals/month

Break-even: 1 deal every 3 months (minimum viability)
```

**Safe Zone:** 2+ deals/quarter (covers fixed costs + reinvestment)
