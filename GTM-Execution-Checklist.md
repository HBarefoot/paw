## Red Flags & Course Correction

### If MQLs < 10/week (Week 4+):
**Diagnosis:**
- Outbound messaging not resonating
- Content not distributing well
- Target account list wrong

**Action:**
- A/B test new subject lines (3 variants)
- Interview 3 non-responders (why no interest?)
- Pivot content topics (survey audience)
- Refine ICP (revenue range, tech stack, pain points)

### If Discovery Calls < 2/week (Week 6+):
**Diagnosis:**
- Outreach volume too low
- Landing page not converting
- No clear CTA

**Action:**
- Double outbound volume (50 → 100/week)
- Add calendar booking link to all content
- Run LinkedIn poll (engagement → DMs)
- Activate 2 more partners

### If Win Rate < 15% (Week 10+):
**Diagnosis:**
- Pricing too high
- Wrong stakeholder in room
- Competition undercutting

**Action:**
- Add "AI Readiness Assessment" ($10K foot-in-door)
- Multi-thread accounts earlier (3+ stakeholders)
- Lead with time-to-value (4-8 weeks vs. 18 months)
- Offer phased implementation (start small)

### If Cash Flow Negative Month 3:
**Diagnosis:**
- Sales cycle longer than expected
- Deals slipping to next quarter
- Marketing spend too high

**Action:**
- Cut paid media by 50% immediately
- Focus on partner-sourced deals (lower CAC)
- Offer 10% discount for upfront payment
- Extend runway with founder bridge

## Templates & Scripts

### Outbound Email Template #1 (Cold)

```
Subject: Quick question about [Company]'s freight audit process

Hi [First Name],

Noticed [Company] runs [TMS/WMS from LinkedIn/research]. Curious: how many hours 
does your team spend weekly on freight bill exceptions?

We just helped a $400M 3PL reduce this from 20 hours to 45 minutes using AI 
infrastructure (not a SaaS platform - custom build for their stack).

If manual exception handling is still a thing, I can share the architecture 
diagram. No pitch, just patterns that work.

Worth a 15-minute chat?

[Founder Name]
Barefoot Digital

P.S. - Here's the case study: [link]
```

### LinkedIn Connection Request

```
[First Name] - saw your post on [topic]. We're working on similar challenges 
with mid-market logistics operators (AI infrastructure for freight audit, 
shipment tracking, etc.). Would love to connect and share notes.

No pitch, just peer learning.
```

### Discovery Call Opening (5 min)

```
"Thanks for making time. Before we dive in, I want to make sure this is 
worth your time.

Here's what I know about [Company]: [2-3 specifics from research].

Here's what I don't know: What's your biggest operational bottleneck right 
now? Where is your team spending time on manual work that should be automated?

If we can't help with that, I'll tell you immediately and we can part ways. 
Fair?"
```

### Proposal Follow-Up (3 days after sending)

```
Hi [First Name],

Wanted to check if you had a chance to review the proposal.

Two questions:
1. Does the scope match what you're looking to solve?
2. Any concerns about timeline or pricing?

Happy to jump on a 15-minute call to walk through anything. Or if this isn't 
a priority right now, totally understand - just let me know so I can follow 
up at the right time.

Best,
[Name]
```
