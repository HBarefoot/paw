# Barefoot Digital Go-to-Market Strategy
## AI Infrastructure Consulting for Mid-Market Logistics Operators

**Tagline:** Automating the Future of Logistics & Fintech with Production-Ready AI

**Target:** Mid-market logistics operators ($100M-$1B revenue)

**Document Date:** June 2025

---

## Executive Summary

Barefoot Digital positions as the engineering-first AI infrastructure partner for logistics operators who need production-ready automation, not pilots. The mid-market segment ($100M-$1B revenue) is underserved: too small for Accenture/Deloitte's premium teams, too complex for generic AI consultants.

**Core Value Proposition:** We ship AI infrastructure that converts days of manual operations to minutes, with measurable efficiency gains (95%+ automation rates) and documented cost savings ($500K+ per implementation).

## Market Analysis

### Target Segment Profile

**Company Characteristics:**
- Revenue: $100M - $1B annually
- Employee count: 500-5,000
- Operations: Multi-node distribution, cross-border shipping, or freight brokerage
- Tech stack: Legacy TMS/WMS (Manhattan, Blue Yonder, Mercury Gate) + custom integrations
- Pain points: Manual exception handling, disconnected systems, reactive operations

**Decision Makers:**
- VP/Director of Operations (economic buyer)
- VP of Technology/CTO (technical evaluator)
- COO (ultimate approver for infrastructure investments)
- Head of Supply Chain (end-user champion)

### Market Dynamics

**Tailwinds:**
1. Labor costs up 23% since 2020 (BLS data)
2. Customer expectations: real-time visibility now standard
3. Legacy system debt: average logistics operator runs 8-12 disconnected platforms
4. AI maturity: LLMs + RAG now reliable for document processing, exception handling, predictive routing

**Competitive Landscape:**
- **Big 4 Consulting:** $500K+ engagements, 6-month timelines, junior-heavy delivery
- **Boutique AI Shops:** Strong tech, weak logistics domain expertise
- **In-house builds:** 18-24 month timelines, high attrition risk
- **SaaS Platforms:** Generic solutions, limited customization, integration gaps

**Barefoot Differentiation:**
- Engineering-led (founders write code, not just decks)
- Production-first (no "pilot purgatory")
- Logistics-specific patterns (we've seen the edge cases)
- 4-8 week implementations vs. 6-month consulting cycles

## CAC/LTV Projections

### Assumptions

**Average Contract Value (ACV):**
- Initial implementation: $150K - $300K (one-time)
- Ongoing support/optimization: $30K - $60K/year (recurring)
- Expansion (additional use cases): $100K - $200K/year 2+

**Sales Cycle:** 45-90 days (mid-market, 3-4 stakeholder approvals)

**Close Rate:** 25% of qualified opportunities (engineering-led differentiation)

### CAC Calculation (Blended)

| Channel | % of Deals | CAC per Deal | Weighted CAC |
|---------|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Direct Outbound | 35% | $45K | $15.75K |
| Content Inbound | 30% | $35K | $10.50K |
| Partner Referrals | 15% | $25K | $3.75K |
| Events | 10% | $60K | $6.00K |
| Paid Media | 10% | $55K | $5.50K |
| **Blended CAC** | | | **$41.5K** |

### LTV Calculation

**Year 1:** $200K (avg. implementation + first year support)

**Year 2:** $80K (support + 1 expansion use case)

**Year 3:** $100K (support + 2nd expansion)

**Gross Margin:** 70% (engineering-heavy, low COGS)

**Retention Rate:** 90% annually (infrastructure is sticky)

**LTV Formula:** (Year1 + Year2×0.9 + Year3×0.81) × 0.70 margin

**LTV =** ($200K + $72K + $65.6K) × 0.70 = **$236.3K**

### LTV:CAC Ratio

**LTV:CAC = $236.3K : $41.5K = 5.7:1**

**Industry Benchmark:** 3:1 is healthy, 5:1+ is exceptional

**Interpretation:** Significant room to increase CAC (hire more AEs, increase paid spend) while maintaining efficient unit economics.

### Payback Period

**Monthly recurring revenue (avg.):** $5K/month ($60K/year support contracts)

**CAC Payback:** $41.5K ÷ $5K/month = **8.3 months**

**Target:** <12 months is excellent for B2B infrastructure

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Long Sales Cycles
**Mitigation:**
- Offer "AI Readiness Assessment" ($10K, 2-week engagement) as foot-in-door
- Create urgency with labor cost data + competitor moves
- Multi-thread accounts early (3+ stakeholders from call 1)

### Risk 2: In-House Build Competition
**Mitigation:**
- Lead with "time to value" math (4-8 weeks vs. 18 months)
- Highlight attrition risk (AI engineers in high demand)
- Offer hybrid model (we build core, their team maintains)

### Risk 3: Economic Downturn
**Mitigation:**
- Frame as cost reduction, not innovation (recession-proof messaging)
- Offer phased implementations (start small, expand)
- Target PE-owned companies (efficiency mandates remain)

### Risk 4: Channel Conflict
**Mitigation:**
- Clear partner rules of engagement (deal registration)
- No direct outreach to partner accounts without approval
- Transparent communication on overlap situations
